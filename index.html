Connect your TMDB key

StreamScout pulls live Netflix, Prime Video and Hotstar availability in India from TMDB, the same catalog data JustWatch uses, free to query with your own key. The key stays in this browser only.

  1. Create a free account at themoviedb.org
  2. Go to Settings, then API, and request a key as a Developer
  3. Paste either the "API Key" or the "API Read Access Token" below - both work
